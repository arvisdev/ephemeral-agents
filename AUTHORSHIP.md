# Authorship & Provenance

**Project:** ephemeral-agents — an organization of AI agents
**Author & originator:** **Ronald A Steinhauer** — A&R Visual Development Inc (arvisdev) · Human-In-The-Loop project owner (HITL)
**Original conception date:** **May 13, 2026**

## 1. Original work of the author

The following are the original work and intellectual contribution of the author, conceived on **May 13, 2026**:

- **The original idea** — the concept of operating AI agents as an *organization* rather than as individuals: durable orchestration, ephemeral single-task workers, a role roster, task-scoped credentials, institutional memory, and human judgment at the system's doors.
- **The architecture and design** — the three-department organizational model (business, engineering, workforce), the durable-core pattern, the credential mechanism, the milestone structure (M0–M5), and every component decision and its rationale.
- **The specifications** — the requirements, behavioral specifications, test procedures, gate rules, delivery plans, and business rules that govern the system.
- **The research direction** — every research area, question, and investigation performed during this project was specified and directed by the author.

## 2. The role of AI

AI (Hermes Agent) served this project as a **directed assistant** — a research assistant, document creator, and implementation tool — in exactly five functions, none of which included originating the idea, the design, or the specifications:

1. **Validation.** The AI tested and verified the author's designs and specifications by executing the proof-of-concept build on real infrastructure. The AI validated; it did not originate.
2. **Document production.** The AI produced the formatted documents in this repository — the design-doc set, this project's reports, and the companion book — from the author's designs, specifications, and directed research.
3. **Code production, under the author's rules.** The AI wrote and tested the Go source code, strictly within the specific architectural constraints, rules, and business rules stated by the author (the Go-for-orchestration decision, the message contract, the durable-core pattern, the ephemeral worker model, the credential rules). The code is an implementation of the author's design and rules, not an independent creative act.
4. **Installation and deployment tooling, per the architectural specifications.** The AI wrote and tested the bash/shell installation scripts and the docker-compose deployment files — as the executable expression of the author's architectural specifications, not as original architectural work.
5. **Build-exercise logs.** The AI generated the operational logs and run records produced while executing the build exercises, under the author's direction.

## 3. Authority and sign-off

Every gate result and test-procedure outcome in this repository carries the
author's sign-off (recorded on the author's behalf under standing written
authorization; the final sign-off marks the **completion of the POC build and
test on 2026-11-07**). That sign-off is the authority under which all
AI-performed work in this project exists: the AI built and verified what the
author specified, and the author accepted the results.
